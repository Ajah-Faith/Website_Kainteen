About KainTayo!

A student-made campus food guide created for CSCORE1L — Introduction to Computing.

← Back to Home

Our Purpose

KainTayo! brings together information about three selected food stalls inside the NU East Ortigas school canteen. It helps students explore their food offerings and prices through a simple, organized, user-friendly website.

The project demonstrates HTML, CSS, multi-page navigation, responsive design, and clear information organization.

Target Users

  • NU East Ortigas students
  • Teachers and school staff
  • Members of the school community
