About the Project

About VCanteen

A student-created website project for CSCORE1L – Introduction to Computing.

Project Purpose

VCanteen was created to organize and present information about selected food stalls within the NU East Ortigas school canteen. The website aims to make it easier for students and other members of the school community to explore food choices in one place.

The project demonstrates basic HTML, CSS, web design, navigation, organization of content, and responsive website development.

Target Users

  • NU East Ortigas students
  • Teachers and school staff
  • Visitors and members of the school community
  • People interested in the featured canteen stalls
