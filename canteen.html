About the Canteen

A shared space for food and breaks.

The NU East Ortigas school canteen brings together different food choices for students and members of the school community.

What is the canteen?

The school canteen is a convenient place where students can find different meals, snacks, and drinks during their school day. With multiple stalls offering different kinds of food, students have several options depending on what they want to eat.

This website highlights three selected stalls: V Coffee, Ahlee's, and Kuya Vinceky.

At a glance

Different stalls, different specialties

Drinks & Coffee

V Coffee offers coffee, non-coffee drinks, cold beverages, and pastries.

Meals & Snacks

Ahlee's offers rice meals and snack choices.

Silog Favorites

Kuya Vinceky offers silog-style meals with egg, rice, and sauces.
